1. Official Contest Rules

1.1 Sponsor

This contest is sponsored by Blockchain Spirits LLC (“Sponsor”). All inquiries regarding these Official Rules should be directed to the Sponsor via the contact form at topkpop.io.

1.2 No Purchase Necessary

No purchase or payment of any kind is necessary to enter or win this contest. A purchase will not increase your chances of winning. This contest is free to enter and free to play.

1.3 Eligibility

The Top K-Pop Super Sleuth contest is open to legal residents of the United States who are currently employed by or affiliated with an accredited K–12 school, school district, or educational institution at the time of entry. Eligible participants include classroom teachers, instructional coaches, curriculum specialists, school administrators, and district-level education professionals. Participants must be at least 18 years of age. To claim the speakeasy dinner prize component, the winner must be 21 years of age or older at the time of prize redemption. Employees, officers, directors, and agents of Blockchain Spirits LLC, and members of their immediate families or households, are not eligible to participate.

1.4 Contest Period

The contest begins when registration opens and ends on the date specified on topkpop.io. Blockchain Spirits LLC reserves the right to extend or modify the contest period with reasonable notice posted on the website.

1.5 How to Enter

To enter, participants must register at topkpop.io/pages/register/ and complete the online registration form. No purchase is required. Limit one registration per person. Duplicate registrations will be disqualified. Participants may play solo or as a team of two educators.

1.5a Participant Cap — First-Come, First-Served

Each game session of Top K-Pop Super Sleuth is limited to a maximum of fifty (50) active participants (counting individuals, not teams). Spots are filled on a strict first-come, first-served basis determined by the date and time of completed registration. Once the 50-participant cap is reached for a given session, registration for that session will close automatically.

Important: Submitting a registration form does not guarantee a spot. Your registration is confirmed only when you receive a confirmation email from Blockchain Spirits LLC. If the session is full at the time your registration is processed, you will be placed on the waitlist for the next available session.

1.5b Waitlist Policy

Registrants who are not admitted to the current session due to the 50-participant cap will be automatically added to a waitlist for the next scheduled game session. Waitlist placement is determined by the order in which registrations were received. Waitlisted participants will be notified by email when registration opens for the next session and will be given priority access before general registration reopens to the public. Being on the waitlist does not guarantee admission to the next session, as each session is subject to the same 50-participant cap. Waitlist status does not carry over indefinitely; Blockchain Spirits LLC reserves the right to clear and reset the waitlist between seasons or program cycles with reasonable notice.

1.6 How to Win — Scoring and Winner Determination

Top K-Pop Super Sleuth is a skill-based contest. Winners are determined by the following criteria, applied in order:

  • Primary criterion: The participant or team that correctly identifies the saboteur AND accumulates the highest total point score wins the Grand Prize.
  • If no participant correctly identifies the saboteur: The participant or team with the highest total point score wins the Grand Prize, regardless of their saboteur guess.
  • Tiebreaker: In the event of a tie in points, the participant or team who submitted their final accusation earliest (by timestamp) will be declared the winner.

Points are earned by completing missions, analyzing evidence, and submitting correct answers throughout the game. The Sponsor’s determination of scores and the winner is final and binding.
