Make a song

Bring the song and your lyrics

For songs sung in English. Is your song in another language? Use MyKaraoke.Video instead, as shown on the Trove 02 page.

The full song with singing, saved as MP3 or M4A, up to 25 MB.

The room listens to the recording and uses only the lines it actually hears, so a longer version or extra verses are fine. No lyrics handy? Leave this empty and the room will go by ear.

Each new line takes the timing of the same line in the original.

Your upload is used to make the track and is not kept. To do this, the song and lyrics are sent to the AI music services the room uses. The music-only track is private to your team, and you can delete it at any time.

Trying it out

Hear it sung

Want to hear your new lyrics before you sing them? A computer voice sings them to the tune of your song. It uses the song file from box 1 and the lyrics from box 3 above. For this part the song file must be MP3 or WAV. The voices are styles, not copies of any real singer.
